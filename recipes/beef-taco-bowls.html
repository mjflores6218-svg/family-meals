Beef Taco Bowls

Seasoned ground beef with rice, black beans, peppers, zucchini, salsa and cheese

👥 2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 20 min⏱ 30 min total~800 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Start ¾ cup rice according to package directions.
2
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add ground beef, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Cook 6–8 minutes, breaking it apart, until browned and cooked through.
3
Add bell pepper and zucchini to the beef skillet. Cook 5–7 minutes until crisp-tender and lightly browned.
4
Warm the black beans in a small saucepan or microwave until hot.
5
Build bowls with rice, beef and vegetables, black beans, salsa and shredded cheese.
6
Finish with a squeeze of lime and serve hot.
