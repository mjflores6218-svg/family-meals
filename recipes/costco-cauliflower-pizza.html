Kirkland Signature Cauliflower-Crust Supreme Pizza

Kirkland Signature supreme cauliflower-crust pizza with pepperoni, sausage, roasted vegetables and a three-cheese blend

👥 2 servings (½ pizza each)Prep 2 minCook 18 min⏱ 20 min total620 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Heat oven to 425°F. Keep the pizza frozen while the oven preheats.
2
Remove the pizza from its overwrap and cardboard. Place the frozen pizza directly on the center oven rack; do not use a baking sheet.
3
Bake for 17–19 minutes, or until the cheese is golden brown. For convection, bake at 425°F for 14–16 minutes.
4
Let the pizza rest for 2–3 minutes, then slice and serve. Add a salad kit on the side if desired.
