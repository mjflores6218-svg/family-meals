Trader Joe’s Mandarin Orange Chicken

Crispy glazed orange chicken over rice with bright green broccoli

👥 2 servings⏱ 25 min

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Start 1 cup rice according to package directions.
2
Cook Mandarin Orange Chicken according to package directions, keeping the sauce separate until the chicken is crisp.
3
While it cooks, heat 1 tsp oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add broccoli and optional bell pepper.
4
Cook vegetables 5–7 minutes until crisp-tender. Add 1–2 Tbsp water and cover briefly if you prefer softer broccoli.
5
Toss the hot chicken with its orange sauce.
6
Serve chicken over rice with a generous portion of broccoli and peppers.
