Chicken & Mushroom Pot Pies

Morgan family chicken pot pies with poached chicken, carrots, leek, cremini mushrooms, corn and a creamy thyme gravy under flaky Pillsbury crust

👥 4 servingsPrep 35 minCook 60 min⏱ 95 min total~900 cal/serving

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Poach chicken gently in simmering water until cooked through, about 15 minutes. Cool slightly, then shred into bite-size pieces; you want about 3 cups.
2
Heat oven to 400°F. In a wide saucepan over medium heat, cook carrots and leek in 1 Tbsp butter plus olive oil, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 15 minutes. Add garlic near the end.
3
Add mushrooms and cook, stirring, until tender and their moisture has cooked off, about 8–10 minutes. Transfer the vegetable mixture to a bowl.
4
Melt the remaining butter in the saucepan. Sprinkle in flour and whisk until smooth; cook a few minutes to remove the raw-flour taste.
5
Slowly whisk in chicken broth until smooth, then whisk in Silk heavy cream. Simmer until thick and gravy-like.
6
Return chicken and vegetables to the sauce. Stir in corn and thyme. Season well with salt and black pepper.
7
Divide filling among 4 oven-safe individual pot-pie dishes or ramekins. Cut refrigerated pie crust to fit each dish with a small overhang.
8
Lay crust over each pie, crimp the edges, cut a few vents, and brush with egg wash.
9
Bake at 400°F until the crust is deeply golden and the filling is bubbling, about 25–30 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
