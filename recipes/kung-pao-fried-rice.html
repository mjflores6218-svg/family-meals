Trader Joe’s Kung Pao Chicken + Vegetable Fried Rice

Trader Joe’s Kung Pao Chicken with easy vegetable fried rice and crisp-tender broccoli

👥 2 servingsPrep 5 minCook 20 min⏱ 25 min total~900 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Preheat a large skillet according to the package directions for the Kung Pao Chicken.
2
Cook the Kung Pao Chicken according to package directions, keeping the sauce separate until the chicken is crisp.
3
In a second skillet, heat the vegetable fried rice over medium-high, stirring occasionally, until hot and steaming.
4
While the frozen items cook, heat 1 tsp oil in the first skillet or a third pan. Add broccoli and 1–2 Tbsp water, cover for 2 minutes, then uncover and cook until crisp-tender.
5
Toss the hot chicken with its sauce and stir the fried rice once more.
6
Serve Kung Pao Chicken with vegetable fried rice and a generous portion of broccoli.
