BBQ Meatloaf & Loaded Potato Wedges

Glazed individual meatloaf with cheesy potato wedges and roasted carrots

👥 2 servingsPrep 15 minCook 35 min⏱ 50 min total~820 cal/serving

All Steps

1
Heat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan for easy cleanup.
2
Toss potato wedges and carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange on the sheet pan and begin roasting.
3
Mix ground meat, panko, egg, onion powder, 2 Tbsp BBQ sauce, ½ tsp salt, and pepper just until combined. Form into two small loaves.
4
After vegetables have roasted about 10 minutes, add meatloaves to the pan (or use a second small pan). Brush tops with BBQ sauce.
5
Roast until meatloaves are cooked through and vegetables are tender, about 20–25 more minutes.
6
Sprinkle cheese over hot potato wedges and return to oven for 1–2 minutes to melt.
7
Rest meatloaf 5 minutes. Serve with loaded wedges, sour cream, roasted carrots, and extra BBQ sauce if desired.
