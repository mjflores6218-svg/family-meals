Aidells Chicken & Apple Sausage Sheet-Pan Dinner

Roasted chicken & apple sausage with baby potatoes, carrots and zucchini

👥 2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 30 min⏱ 40 min total~650 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Heat oven to 425°F. Halve the baby potatoes, cut the carrots into chunks, and slice the zucchini.
2
Toss potatoes and carrots with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper on a sheet pan. Roast for 15 minutes.
3
Add the sliced Aidells chicken & apple sausage and zucchini to the pan. Toss with the roasting vegetables.
4
Roast 12–15 minutes more, until the potatoes are tender, carrots are browned, zucchini is tender, and sausage is heated through.
5
Broil for 1–2 minutes if you want extra browning, watching closely.
6
Divide between two plates and serve immediately.
