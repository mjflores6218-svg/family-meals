Chicken Sausage Rigatoni

Rigatoni coated in tomato sauce with browned chicken sausage, Parmesan and roasted broccoli

👥 2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 20 min⏱ 30 min total~760 cal/serving
Tap to rate
Original Recipe

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Heat oven to 425°F. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil.
2
Toss broccoli with a little olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 15–18 minutes until browned at the edges.
3
Boil rigatoni until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
4
Meanwhile, brown sliced chicken sausage in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes.
5
Add marinara and Italian seasoning to the sausage. Simmer 3–4 minutes.
6
Add drained rigatoni. Toss with Parmesan, adding a splash of pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
7
Serve rigatoni with roasted broccoli and extra Parmesan.
