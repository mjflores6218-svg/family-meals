Chicken Enchiladas with Tomatillo Sauce

Two bubbling enchiladas with tomatillo sauce and melted cheese, with roasted corn and zucchini

👥 2 servingsPrep 15 minCook 25 min⏱ 40 min total~720 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Heat oven to 400°F. Prepare the enchiladas using the linked original recipe as your primary guide.
2
Use about 2 cups cooked shredded chicken for the filling; this can come from the chicken purchased for the week.
3
Assemble enchiladas with chicken, tomatillo sauce, tortillas, and cheese according to the original recipe.
4
Toss corn and sliced zucchini with a little olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
5
Bake the enchiladas until hot and bubbling; roast the vegetables alongside until browned-tender.
6
Rest enchiladas 5 minutes before serving. Plate with the roasted corn and zucchini.
