Chicken Sausage Rigatoni with Mushrooms & Zucchini

Rigatoni with browned chicken sausage, cremini mushrooms and zucchini in tomato sauce with Parmesan

👥 2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 20 min⏱ 30 min total~760 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Bring a pot of well-salted water to a boil. Slice the sausage, zucchini and mushrooms; mince the garlic.
2
Cook rigatoni until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
3
Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Brown sausage 4–5 minutes.
4
Add mushrooms and zucchini. Cook 5–7 minutes until browned-tender; add garlic for the final 30 seconds.
5
Add marinara and Italian seasoning. Simmer 3–4 minutes.
6
Add rigatoni and Parmesan. Toss, adding a splash of pasta water if needed to make the sauce glossy.
7
Serve hot with extra Parmesan if desired.
