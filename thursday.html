Argentine-Style Chicken & Chimichurri

Seared chicken with bright chimichurri, charred sweet corn and browned zucchini

👥 2 servingsPrep 15 minCook 20 min⏱ 35 min total~560 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Make chimichurri: combine parsley, garlic, red wine vinegar, 2 Tbsp olive oil, oregano, red pepper flakes and a pinch of salt.
2
Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat a large skillet or grill pan over medium-high.
3
Cook chicken until browned and 165°F in the center, about 5–7 minutes per side depending on thickness. Rest 5 minutes.
4
While chicken cooks, brush corn and zucchini lightly with the remaining olive oil; season with salt and pepper.
5
Char corn and zucchini in a hot skillet or grill pan until browned and tender, turning as needed, about 8–10 minutes.
6
Slice chicken and spoon chimichurri over the top. Serve with charred corn and zucchini.
