Street Cart-Style Chicken Bowls

Spiced chicken and zucchini over jasmine rice with a bright lime sauce, served with a simple salad kit

👥 2 servingsPrep 15 minCook 15 min⏱ 30 min total~700 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Cook 1 cup jasmine rice according to package directions and keep covered.
2
Season chicken with cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir together sour cream, juice from half the lime, and 1–2 tsp water for a quick sauce.
3
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook chicken until browned and cooked through, about 6–8 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
4
Add zucchini to the same skillet and cook until browned-tender, about 5–6 minutes.
5
Divide rice between bowls and add chicken and zucchini. Drizzle with the lime sauce and remaining cilantro.
6
Serve with a simple salad kit on the side; use the included dressing only on the portion that wants it.
