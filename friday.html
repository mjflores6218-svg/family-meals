Homemade Uncured Pepperoni Pizza

Golden thin-crust pizza with melted mozzarella and uncured pepperoni beside a fresh salad

👥 2 servings⏱ 35 min

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Take pizza dough out of the refrigerator 20–30 minutes before shaping. Heat oven as hot as practical, ideally 475–500°F, with a sheet pan or pizza stone preheating.
2
On a lightly floured surface, stretch dough into a 12–14 inch round or oval.
3
Spread with pizza sauce, leaving a border. Add mozzarella, then a full base layer of uncured pepperoni. Add optional vegetables.
4
Transfer to the hot pan or stone. Bake until crust is browned and cheese is bubbling, usually 10–14 minutes.
5
While pizza bakes, toss the salad kit.
6
Rest pizza 2–3 minutes, slice, and serve with salad.
