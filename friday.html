Turkey Burgers with Tomato-Cucumber Salad & Corn

Juicy seasoned turkey burgers on toasted buns with a crisp tomato-cucumber salad and sweet corn

👥 2 servingsPrep 15 minCook 15 min⏱ 30 min total~650 cal/serving
Tap to rate

Ingredients

All Steps

1
Dice half the tomato and the cucumber; thinly slice red onion. Toss with 1 tsp olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt and pepper. Slice the remaining tomato for the burgers.
2
Mix ground turkey gently with Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder, ½ tsp salt and pepper. Form two large patties (or three smaller patties and freeze one uncooked).
3
Heat a skillet or grill pan over medium-high with a light film of oil. Cook patties 5–6 minutes per side until browned and 165°F in the center.
4
At the same time, cook corn in a hot skillet/grill pan, turning until tender and lightly charred, about 8–10 minutes.
5
Toast buns cut-side down for 1–2 minutes.
6
Build burgers with sauce, lettuce if using, sliced tomato and turkey patties. Serve with tomato-cucumber salad and corn.
